# Sales checklist

Before showing a deliverable. A failed line is fixed or stays an open question.

- `positioning_blob` is the validated positioning's blob. One goal, one audience; one action per
  page, one idea per email.
- Every claim, price, term and figure has a row (**Evidence** or **Claims**) with status and
  source: `verified` names a current base page; `stated` carries the creator's date; `untrusted`
  names its source and is a lead, never a promise.
- **No price of your own.** `free`, `none`, or `stated` with the creator's amount and date; a
  discount, trial, guarantee or refund follows the same rule. No testimonial, customer, rating,
  award, deadline or limited quantity unless `stated` or proved by the base.
- Nothing promised that the product cannot do; objections get an honest answer.
- Offer: two headlines; what is included, for whom and not; `pricing` matches the text; one call
  to action.
- Sequence: header and body list the same emails, subjects as in the header; the first says who
  writes and why; no fake "re:", invented urgency or earlier conversation; seven
  emails at most (`docs check` warns), a day apart at least; `opt_out` and `stop_condition`;
  **Before you send** filled, "not legal advice".
- Header `id`, `kind: note`, `owner: milo`, `links`; no status or cost; no comment, generated line
  or placeholder; `npx agentplugged docs check` clean; the `publication` cites the committed blob.

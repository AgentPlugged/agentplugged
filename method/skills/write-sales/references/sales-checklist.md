# Sales checklist

Walk through it before showing an offer page or a sequence to the creator. A failed line is
fixed, or it stays visible as an open question.

## Starting point

- The positioning was validated when you started (its last approved `publication` checkpoint
  cites the very blob of the file), and `positioning_blob` in the header is that blob.
- The offer and the audience come from the positioning and the knowledge base, not from memory
  and not from the repository.
- Each deliverable has **one goal and one audience**, named in its header, and the text serves
  them: one action asked per page, one idea per email.

## Grounding

- Every claim, price, term and figure of the text has a row (in **Evidence** for an offer, in
  **Claims** for a sequence), with its status and its source.
- Each `verified` row names a page of the knowledge base that says it, and that page is current.
- Each `stated` row carries the date of the creator's statement and is not worded as proven.
- Each `untrusted` row names the source it was read from and is a lead the creator confirms,
  never a promise of the text.
- **No price of your own.** The price is `free` (the base says the product is free), `none` (the
  page names none), or `stated` with the creator's amount and the date of their decision. A
  discount, a trial, a guarantee, a refund term follow the same rule.
- **No testimonial, customer name, customer count, rating, award, "most popular", deadline or
  limited quantity** unless the creator gave it to you (a `stated` row) or the base proves it.
- What the product cannot do, or does only in part, is not promised; the limits the base states
  are respected, and an objection gets an honest answer, even when the answer is "not yet".

## An offer page

- The headline answers what the visitor came for in the first lines, in two variants with their
  reasons; the promise is the positioning's, no more.
- It says what is included, for whom, and for whom not.
- The `pricing` field of the header matches the text: a price in the text is `stated`; no price
  in the text is `free` or `none`.
- One call to action, and where it leads.
- The page is text the creator copies as it is; what the site must change to host it is left to
  a developer story, never done by you.

## An email sequence

- The header lists every email with its subject and its delay, and the body has the same
  sections in the same order, each starting with the subject as written in the header.
- The first email says who writes and why this person. A follow-up refers to something that
  happened (the person's own action) and never pretends to a conversation that did not.
- No false "re:" or "fwd:", no invented urgency, no hidden sender, no promise the product cannot
  keep.
- Short enough to read on a phone; one call to action per email.
- As few emails as the goal needs (`docs check` warns beyond seven) and a delay that respects
  the reader: a day at least, more when the goal is not urgent.
- `opt_out` says how every email lets the reader stop, and the emails say it too; `stop_condition`
  says when the sequence stops for a recipient.
- **Before you send** holds the reminders of `references/before-you-send.md`, with one line per
  country of the recipients when the creator named them, and says it is not legal advice.
- Nothing was sent, and nothing was loaded into a sending tool.

## Form

- Written in the product's language, headings in the creator's; `language` names the product's.
- Header: `id: offer-<slug>` or `id: sequence-<slug>`, `kind: note`, `owner: milo`, `links`
  holding `positioning`, `language`, `sales`. No status, no cost, no progress.
- No requirement identifier opens a line, a list item, a heading or a first table cell.
- No guidance comment, no "Generated by agentplugged" line and no placeholder is left.
- `npx agentplugged docs check` reports no problem, and the warnings are read.

## Validation

- The deliverable is committed, and its `publication` checkpoint cites the committed blob.
- It is ready for the creator to validate; validating it publishes and sends nothing.

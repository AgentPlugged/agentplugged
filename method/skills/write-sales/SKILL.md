## Overview

- **Step:** launch, as Milo (`agent: milo` in every call).
- **Produces:** an offer page (`assets/offer-template.md`) or an email sequence
  (`assets/sequence-template.md`), one goal, one audience.
- **Reads:** the validated positioning, `launch/narrative.md` if validated, the base pages.
- **Validation point:** a `publication` checkpoint per deliverable.

## Before you start

- Milo not active in `state.get`, or the `sales` domain off: say so, `partial`.

## Decisions

- **Prices and terms are the creator's call, always.** A price, discount, guarantee, trial,
  customer, testimonial, figure or deadline no base page states is a `decision` (`type: decision`,
  options `provide`, `ask_base`, `leave_out`; a price `none` too). Mark `[open: G-…]` at the end of
  the sentence.
- **Apply:** once resolved, read its `resolution` in full (`state.get { checkpoint: "<id>" }`) and apply it as "Applying a resolution" says (reference `skill-rules`); remove the mark. A decided price: `pricing.status: stated`, **Evidence** as `stated`.

## Steps

1. **Start.** `agent.status` `working`; `state.get`; `npx agentplugged next --json`. Phase not
   `launch`, or `next` names another step: say so, `partial`.
2. **Positioning.** Read `.agentplugged/docs/launch/positioning.md`, `.agentplugged/docs/index.md`. **The positioning must be validated** (step 10) to write: absent,
   draft or changed since approval, you write nothing; Julien writes it (when Julien is not
   active, say so too), `partial`. Read the `sales.positioning_blob` of every deliverable that exists.
   Without `index.md` there is no knowledge base: Zoé writes it first, `partial`. Intent: **offer**,
   **sequence** or **review** (needs no validated positioning: `references/sales-checklist.md`,
   `docs check`, `partial`, no checkpoint). After a refusal read its `checkpoint.resolution.note`.
3. **Ground the claims** in the base; ask the creator's facts once (`stated`, dated). A missing fact that weighs is a `decision`; a missing price is always one.
4. **Write the offer** in `.agentplugged/docs/sales/offers/<slug>.md`, without
   the "Generated by agentplugged" line and comments. Header: `goal`, `audience`,
   `positioning_blob`, `language` (the product's), `pricing` (`free`, `none`, or `stated` with its `amount`
   and `stated_on`, never otherwise). Text: two headlines, what is included, for whom and not,
   objections, one call to action. **Evidence:** a row per claim, price and term (`verified`,
   `stated`, `untrusted`).
5. **Review gate (offer).** `references/sales-checklist.md`, `npx agentplugged docs check` clean.
6. **Write the sequence** in `.agentplugged/docs/sales/sequences/<slug>.md`.
   Header: `type`, `opt_out`, `stop_condition`, `emails` (`subject`, `delay_days`); one `## Email n`
   each, one call to action. **Before you send** (`references/before-you-send.md`): one line per
   country, never a legal opinion. **Claims**: a row per claim. Never send, never load the
   sequence into a tool, never ask for access to one.
7. **Review gate (sequence).** As step 5.
8. **Decision gate.** Every `decision` you opened resolved; else no publication, `partial`.
9. **Commit and open the publication.** `git branch --show-current` names a branch (else ask, open
   nothing); `git commit` the deliverable; `checkpoint.open` `type: publication`, medium risk, no story, `documents` the deliverable alone (`path`, `branch`, `blob_sha` from
   `git rev-parse HEAD:<path>`). One checkpoint per deliverable; `supersedes` after a refusal. Ask
   once whether it suits them: `chat.answer`; approving it sends nothing, the creator publishes.
10. **Validated or not.** The last *approved* `publication` checkpoint citing the positioning
    (`approved` or `approved_with_reservations`; never a waiver, never an option picked) has a
    `blob_sha` equal to the blob of the file as it is now. An older `positioning_blob`: stale.
11. **Report.** `story.result` (`complete` once the publication is open, else `partial`).

## Rules

- Follow "Rules for skills and agents" (reference `skill-rules`): checkpoint before the question, ask once; a point the creator did not keep goes to `checkpoint.decide`; in French say « tu »; start with `memory.read`.
- Produce nothing without a validated positioning, and say so.
- Write only the files of `.agentplugged/docs/sales/offers/` and `.agentplugged/docs/sales/sequences/`.
- Never publish, send, schedule, buy or pay anything. Never send an email, never load a sequence
  or an address into a sending tool, never contact a prospect; no fake "re:" nor false scarcity.
- Never invent a price, a discount, a guarantee, a testimonial, a customer, a figure or a
  deadline: a statement of the creator, an `untrusted` source, or a question.
- Declare every read outside the product with `guard.external_read` (`tool`, `source`) before you read; keep it marked `untrusted`.
- Do not open the end of the launch phase on your own. Never call another agent.

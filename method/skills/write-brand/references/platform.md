# The brand platform (Julien)

Read this before writing `brand.md`, from `assets/brand-template.md`; `docs check` reads the shape.
Draw it from the brief, the positioning and the research pages (quoted, the page in `links`); ask
the creator only the feeling the product should leave, brands they admire and refuse, and why.

## The seven sections

- **Mission**: why the product exists, for whom; one or two sentences.
- **Values**: three to five, each with the behaviour that proves it.
- **Personality**: three or four traits, each with what it is not (« warm, not chatty »).
- **Promise**: one sentence alone on its line, at most 200 characters, one the product can keep
  (the positioning's, never a bigger one).
- **What the brand is not**: two or more things it is mistaken for, each with why not.
- **Voice and tone**: at least three concrete rules, then a table **To say | To avoid** of at least
  two rows, both cells filled. `docs check` warns when a page of the site or a content uses a phrase
  of the « To avoid » column: write them as they would really appear.
- **Name and slogan**: only when missing, two to four candidates with what each says; availability
  (domain, trademark) says **to verify**, what and where, never « free ».

Name, slogan and voice weigh on the whole product: show options with their cost; if the creator
wants time, open a `decision` checkpoint. Never decide for them.

# The brand platform (Julien)

Read this before writing `brand.md`. It follows `assets/brand-template.md`; `docs check` reads the shape.

## Draw it from what exists

Start from the brief, the positioning and the research pages: the audience, the promise, what rivals
say. Quote them, with the page in `links`. Ask the creator only what none of them says: the feeling
the product should leave, brands they admire and why, brands they refuse and why.

## The seven sections

- **Mission**: why the product exists, for whom. One or two sentences.
- **Values**: three to five, each with the behaviour that proves it (« simple: one screen per decision »).
- **Personality**: three or four traits, each with what it is not (« warm, not chatty »).
- **Promise**: one sentence, alone on its line, at most 200 characters, a promise the product can keep
  (the positioning's, never a bigger one).
- **What the brand is not**: two or more things it is mistaken for, each with why not.
- **Voice and tone**: at least three concrete rules (person, length of sentence, vocabulary, humour, how
  an error is told), then a table **To say | To avoid** of at least two rows, both cells filled, the same
  idea in the right and in the wrong voice. `docs check` warns when a page of the site or a content uses a
  phrase of the « To avoid » column: write those phrases as they would really appear.
- **Name and slogan**: only when missing, two to four candidates, each with what it says. The
  availability column (domain, trademark) says **to verify** and what to check and where; never « free ».

## Choices

Name, slogan and voice weigh on the whole product: show options side by side with their cost, let the
creator choose; when they want time, open a `decision` checkpoint. Do not decide in their place.

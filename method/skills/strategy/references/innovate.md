# Innovation strategy

Evidence over hope; a plan a solo creator can carry. Choose
two to four light frameworks per step, say in a line what each reveals. The sections of the template
are the steps; a step not run keeps its section with one line saying why. By need: no model yet,
steps 1 to 3 then 7; a price decision, 3 and 7. Then `gestures.md`.

1. **Context**. 2. **Market**: reuse `docs research`; every size or share carries its source or
   `[to verify]`. 3. **Model**: revenue and costs only named here. 4. **Openings**.
5. **Options**: five to ten concrete moves, near and far.
6. **Direction**: three different options, judged on fit, timing, defensibility, feasibility, risk
   against reward; recommend one, with the hypotheses to prove first. The creator decides.
7. **Economics**: `costs.md`. 8. **Roadmap**: three phases, no dates, a decision gate between them.
9. **Risks**: early signals, go and no-go gates, plan B.

Frameworks:
- Market: market size in three rings; five forces; outside forces; timing; positioning map.
- Model: business model canvas; value fit; model patterns; revenue ideas; cost redesign.
- Openings: disruption from below; jobs to be done; blue ocean; crossing the gap; platform.
- Options: three horizons; build, measure, learn; ambition grid; strategic intent; scenarios; value chain;
  unbundling; ecosystem design; make or buy; partnerships.
- Technology: adoption curve; S-curve; technology roadmap; open innovation.

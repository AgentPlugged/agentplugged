# From conclusions to gestures

At the end of the file, list in a few lines what the work concluded: the cause found, the option chosen,
the bets under the strategy. For each, ask the creator once which gesture they want. They may answer
some now and the rest later. Never choose for them.

- **Hypothesis followed.** A bet that must be proved (a cause not yet verified, an option that may not
  work, a market claim, a price): `review.critique` with `kind: idea`, `lenses: ["pre_mortem"]`, a
  `summary` (what the file is), and at most twelve findings, one per bet (`severity`, `claim` in one line of 200 characters at most,
  `passage` a label of 120 at most that cites the `EF-n` or `AD-n` the bet touches, so that it comes back when
  a story citing it is delivered, `argument` of ten characters or more, and unless the bet is minor
  `proposal` and `proof`, what would prove or refute it). An earlier idea report not yet sorted
  (`critique_unsorted`) is sorted first. Then the gestures
  and the calls of the skill `critique-deliverable`
  (`.agentplugged/skills/critique-deliverable/references/gestures.md`): keep as a hypothesis, drop with
  their reason, or correct the file. A hypothesis kept comes back when its proof arrives; close it with
  their words only.
- **Story proposed.** One line each under `## Plan` or `## Roadmap` (`Story proposed:` the change, the
  reason, the source). Hand the kept ones to Noah in your closing line: a new requirement goes to Maya, a
  story to cut to Izzy. Write no story and no requirement yourself.
- **Decision point.** A choice between two to four options that only they can make (the direction, the
  price model, the fix): `checkpoint.open` with `type: decision`, the options, your recommended one and
  the file in `documents`; ask once. Record the answer in the file once it is given.
- **Nothing.** The file is enough. Some conclusions are only for the creator to think about.

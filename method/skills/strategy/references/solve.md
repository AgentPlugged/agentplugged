# Solving a problem

Diagnose before you solve. The sections of the
template are the steps; a step not run keeps its section with one line saying why. By need: a bug
that returns, steps 1 to 4; options known, 5 to 8. Then `gestures.md`.

1. **Problem**: one statement of the gap, who lives it, what it costs, what success is.
2. **Boundaries**: where, when, who, what it is and is not.
3. **Causes**: symptom to root, root apart from contributing factors; mark each `[verified]` (seen in
   code, a log, a number) or `[to verify]` (guessed).
4. **Forces**: what drives, what holds back, real limits against assumed.
5. **Options**: at least ten, a few wild; theirs first.
6. **Choice**: criteria first, with them; recommend one with reason, confidence, worries. They decide.
7. **Plan**: actions in order, who does each. No dates.
8. **Checks**: measures, the trial first, plan B, the sign to change course. 9. **Lessons**: optional.

Methods, two or three per step, chosen for the problem (you know them):
- Causes: statement sharpening; is / is not; five whys; cause map; systems view.
- Forces: force field; vital few (80/20); gap analysis; bottleneck search; failure modes.
- Options: contradiction matrix (TRIZ); lateral thinking; morphological grid; nature analogy;
  analogy chain (synectics); assumption busting; random word; reverse brainstorm; six hats; seven lenses.
- Choice: weighted matrix; cost and benefit; risk grid; trial protocol; feasibility.

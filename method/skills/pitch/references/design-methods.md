# Exercises by step

Offer three to five that fit the need, the access to users and the product (digital, service,
physical); say when each works best. One line each: what it is, what to ask.

**Empathy**
- Interviews: a recent real episode, "walk me through it", what frustrates, what would ease it.
- Empathy map: says, thinks, does, feels; the contradictions are the finding.
- Shadowing: watch without helping; note the workarounds and what is never said.
- Journey map: start, steps, struggles, delights, the emotional curve; mark the worst moment.
- Diary: users note moments over days; authentic and slow, for a long cycle.
- Five whys: on the worst moment, until the cause is not a symptom.

**Definition**
- Problem framing: who, what really, why it matters, what success is.
- Point of view: user, need, insight in one sentence.
- How might we: open questions, neither too wide nor already a solution.
- Clustering: group the notes, name each group, read the story they tell.
- Jobs to be done: the functional, emotional and social job the user hires a solution for, and the alternatives.

**Ideas**
- Open brainstorm: quantity, no judging, be visual.
- Eight sketches in eight minutes: forces past the obvious.
- Seven lenses (substitute, combine, adapt, modify, other use, remove, reverse) on what exists.
- Provocation: the most extreme version, to find what matters.
- Analogy: another field or nature already solved this; what to borrow.
- Worst idea turned around.

**Prototype**
- Paper or text flow: rough on purpose, core concept only.
- Role play: act the scenario, where is it awkward.
- Fake backend: a person does by hand what the product will do.
- Storyboard: scenes in sequence, with the emotional curve.
- Physical mock-up: for touch, size, ergonomics.

**Test**
- Task test: "try to do X", think aloud, do not help.
- Feedback grid: liked, questions, ideas, changes.
- Two versions side by side: which serves better, and why.
- Assumption test: list assumptions, test the riskiest, say what would prove it wrong.
- Quick loop: fix, retest.

**Next round**
- Small real pilot. Service blueprint: what the user sees, what happens behind, the handoffs. Stakeholder
  walk-through of the research and prototype. A measurement plan: metrics and when to revisit.

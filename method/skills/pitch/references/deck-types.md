# Kinds of talk

Ask which, then shape the same `deck.md`.

- **Product deck** (users, partners): 6 to 8 slides. Situation, pain, the product at work, the proof, how
  to start.
- **Investor deck**: 10 to 12. Problem, who has it, why now, product, proof so far (every figure sourced),
  market, model, team, the ask and what it buys. A number is the creator's or `[to verify]`.
- **Conference talk or workshop**: large type, few words; the speaker notes carry the talk, with timing
  cues the creator sets; hook, build, payoff; for a workshop, a slide per exercise with its instruction.
- **Video explainer**: slides are scenes. Name the hook at the opening, then a change of picture or a
  new question every 15 to 30 seconds; each scene has `Visual:` and the spoken text in `Notes:`.
- **Single visual** (infographic, one image that explains the idea): one slide whose `Visual:` line
  states the metaphor and the one thing to grasp; the notes say why it works.
- **Concept diagram** (journey, process, a metaphor): one slide per stage, `Visual:` describing the drawing.

For all: story shape (hook, tension, payoff), clarity before cleverness, white space, a consistent look.

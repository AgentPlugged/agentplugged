# Design thinking

Judge late; a failed test is information. Start from what the documents
say of the user (PRD `PU-n` and the personas of `ux.md`, each read by section with `docs read`; research); with no research mark the creator's word `[to verify]`. The sections of the template are the steps; a step not
run keeps its section with one line saying why. By need: unclear problem, steps 0 to 2; known
problem, 3 to 5; a built idea, 5 to 7.

0. **Challenge**: the problem, who, constraints, success; one challenge sentence opens Definition.
1. **Empathy**. 2. **Definition**: "<user> needs <need> because <insight>", 3 to 5 "How might we".
3. **Ideas**: 15 to 30, then the creator keeps two or three. 4. **Prototype**: the least that tests
the riskiest assumption; say what it leaves out. 5. **Test**: five to seven people, one task each;
results are the creator's words, dated, never invented. 6. **Next round**: priority action, one
metric, one date. 7. **Implementation** (`references/design-implementation.md`).

Exercises, three to five per step, chosen for the need and the access to users (you know them):
- Empathy: interviews; empathy map; shadowing; journey map; diary; five whys.
- Definition: problem framing; point of view; how might we; clustering; jobs to be done.
- Ideas: open brainstorm; eight sketches; seven lenses; provocation; analogy.
- Prototype: paper flow; role play; fake backend; storyboard; mock-up.
- Test: task test; feedback grid; two versions; assumption test.
- Next round: pilot; service blueprint; measurement plan.

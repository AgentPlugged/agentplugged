# Structures, by purpose

Each: the beats, as questions. Recommend by purpose and audience; for a product, users: customer journey,
partners: positioning or vision, investors: pitch or fundraising.

**Transformation**
- Hero's path: who, their ordinary world, what disrupts it, the trials, the change, what they bring back.
- Spine: once there was, every day, until one day, because of that, until finally.
- Customer journey: the struggle before, the discovery, the use, the change, the new normal.
- Obstacle overcome: what blocked, how stakes rose, the darkest moment, the breakthrough, the lesson.
- Personal arc: who at the start, what forces change, what they resist, what shifts them, who now.

**Strategic**
- Brand: the spark, the values, the effect on people, the difference, where it goes.
- Vision: today's reality, the opening, the bold picture, the path, the changed future.
- Origin: the spark moment, early struggles, the breakthrough, how it grew, today's mission.
- Positioning: the gap in the market, why us, what is different, why care, the future enabled.
- Culture: principles, behaviours that show them, stories that prove them, how it feels inside.

**Persuasive**
- Pitch: the problem landscape, the solution, the proof, the size of the opportunity, the ask.
- Sales: the pain they feel, that you understand it, the transforming solution, the expected results, the way.
- Change: why we cannot stay, what better is, the stake of not acting, how, what is in it for them.
- Fundraising: the problem that moves, the hope, the impact of the gift, why now, how to help.
- Cause: the wrong, who bears it, the change needed, what happens if we act, how to join.

**Analytical**
- Data: the context, the revelation, the patterns, so what, the action.
- Case: the situation, the approach, the difficulties, the results, what transfers.
- Research: the question, the method, the finding, the meaning, the implications.
- Insight: what everyone assumed, what you noticed, the deeper pattern, why it matters, what changes.
- Making-of: what was built, the choice, the obstacles, how they were solved, what was learned.

**Emotional**
- Hook: the surprising opening, the urgent question, the peaks, the common ground, the payoff.
- Conflict: who wants what, what stands in the way, the escalation, the resolution.
- Empathy: whose view, what they live, what they feel, why the audience should care, the common ground.
- Human interest: the person, their stakes, the universal theme, the journey, what makes it relatable.
- Candid: the hard truth, the struggle, the lesson, why now, the hope.

# Choosing between candidates

For "which one": a stack, a supplier, a tool. The type's sources and window still apply. The page
has three more sections, which `docs check` replays; its header has `shape: choice`.

1. **The frame, before any search.** With the creator, in a few lines: what the winner must do
   (gates) and what they would like (preferences, each weighted 1 to 5). The creator sets the
   weights in their own words; the requirements come from them and the project, never from the
   web. A frame they already gave is restated, not asked again.
2. **The field.** The credible names, narrowed to 3 to 6 finalists; whoever fails a gate is out,
   with the reason. The sources that narrow the field are under 6 months old.
3. **Evidence.** One claim per cell that matters, from the type's sources: the current price page,
   the current version, independent accounts rather than the seller's pitch. Where seller and
   users disagree, that is a finding. The cost over the product's life and what leaving would cost
   are preferences like the others.
4. **The sections** (titles in the creator's language; `gate`, `weighted`, `pass`, `fail` stay as
   written here). `## Criteria`: `Id | Criterion | Weight | Kind`, ids `C-1`, a gate has no weight.
   `## Matrix`: one row per candidate and criterion, `Candidate | Criterion | Score | Evidence`,
   the score 0 to 5 (`pass` or `fail` for a gate), the evidence the numbers of claims (`1, 3`); a
   pass or a 4 or 5 needs a claim that is not in doubt. `## Verdict`. Never add up the totals yourself.
5. **Check, then the verdict.** `docs check` prints the matrix replayed: the ranking, who a gate
   cut, which change of one weight would change the top. Write the verdict from it, one line each
   (`Choix`, `Second`, `Contre`, `Repli`, `Dérogation` are read too): `Pick:` the top, or the
   reason why not in `Override:`; `Runner-up:` and when it would win; `Against:` what speaks most
   against the pick; `Hedge:` how to go back at the lowest cost (a seam, a pilot). `none` when
   nothing survives (pick) or only one candidate does (runner-up). Tell the creator the pick, the
   runner-up, and how much the top depends on their weights.

A choice older than two quarters is searched again before it is relied on.

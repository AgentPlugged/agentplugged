# Choosing between candidates

"Which one" (a stack, a supplier): header `shape: choice`, three more sections, replayed by `docs check`.

1. **The frame, before any search**, with the creator: gates (what the winner must do) and
   preferences weighted 1 to 5; never from the web.
2. **The field**: 3 to 6 finalists; whoever fails a gate is out, with the reason.
3. **Evidence**: one claim per cell, current price page, independent accounts over the seller's pitch.
4. **Sections**: `## Criteria` (ids `C-1`, kind `gate` without weight or `weighted`), `## Matrix`
   (candidate, criterion, score 0 to 5 or `pass`/`fail`, evidence the claim numbers; a pass or a 4 or
   5 needs a claim not in doubt), `## Verdict`. Never add up the totals yourself.
5. `docs check` prints the ranking, who a gate cut, which weight change would change the top. Write
   one line each (`Choix`, `Second`, `Contre`, `Repli`, `Dérogation` are read too): `Pick:` (or why
   not, in `Override:`), `Runner-up:` and when it wins, `Against:`, `Hedge:`; `none` when nothing
   survives or only one does. Tell the creator how much the pick depends on their weights.

# Types of research

Types go in `research.types`; several may apply. Each has a window (an older source backs no claim)
and claims to cross-check (mark them `key`).

- `market`: size, growth, segments, prices; filings, statistics, regulators, sellers' price pages (a
  price never comes from a comparison article). Window: 18 months, a price 3. Cross-check: sizes,
  growth, a rival's traction.
- `domain`: rules, actors, regulations; the law itself. Window: 3 years, a rule in force
  1. Cross-check: every legal obligation.
- `technical`: limits, versions, advisories, costs; official documentation, release notes, the
  repository. Give the version or date a fact holds for. Window: 1 year, a version or price 3
  months. Cross-check: limits, compatibility, security, prices.
- `competitive`: offer, price, shortfalls, one sub-question per competitor; their own site, price
  page, changelog. Window: 6 months, a price 3. Cross-check: prices, traction.
- `user_voice`: public posts, an opinion not a fact: count what you read ("9 of the 30
  reviews read"), never write "users want". Quote without the author's name, handle, e-mail or
  profile link. Window: 2 years.
- `literature`: the paper itself (publisher, DOI or preprint; say which), authors and year in the
  claim. No window; a newer paper that contradicts it is a finding.

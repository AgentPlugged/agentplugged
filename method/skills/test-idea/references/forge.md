# The forge

**Concept.** Name the kind early and write it in the header (`concept:`); ask only if unclear. It
decides who the reader is: `commercial` (a buyer: price, alternatives, first customers),
`internal` (colleagues: effort to adopt, sponsor, upkeep; a tool for oneself is this), `open-source`
(users and contributors: maintainer, licence), `community` (members: who steers, how it lasts).
Begin with the person, not the product: who has the trouble, what they do today. With no reader
after two or three exchanges, go back to the forge or to a round of ideas (skill `ideation`).

**Start.** The idea, what they want (understand, test, improve), new or a change to a project with
documents (`scope: change`, those documents in `links`; they decide).
Offer to resume a forge file with no `Outcome:` line. Say they can steer (*attack*, *defend*, *switch roles*). Copy `assets/forge-template.md` to `.agentplugged/docs/ideas/forge-<slug>.md`.

**Questioning.** One question per turn, the one the others rest on first, with your guess to accept or
correct. Read the documents instead of asking. No flattery, no easy agreement.
Each turn, one persona fitted to the point (`personas.md`, played live) and one outsider you make up.
*attack*: hunt contradictions and ways it fails, no approval until called off; *defend*: the best
case; *switch roles*: swap.

**Log.** After each beat a line under `## Log`, in their meaning, typed `[chose]`, `[bet]`
(believed, unproved), `[gap]`, `[out]`, `[fixed]`, `[path]`.

**Close** with `Outcome:` under the title, log deleted, at most 40 lines: *hardened* (`## Decisions`,
`## Rejected` with reasons; the brief starts here), *killed* (`## Cause`), *clearer* (`## Clearer`).
What stays `[bet]` or `[gap]` becomes bets (`personas.md`).

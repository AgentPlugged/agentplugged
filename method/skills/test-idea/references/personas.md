# Personas and bets

Compose 3 to 5 personas who would sink this idea, chosen for it (a demanding customer, the
competitor, a skeptical investor, whoever pays or decides), each with a name, a stake, a question.
Played in turn, in a fresh context: two or three objections the idea cannot answer each. Merge: **strong objections**, **what holds**, **risky
bets**; show that synthesis in a few lines.

Report: `review.critique` as `.agentplugged/skills/critique-deliverable/references/format.md` says,
with `kind: idea`, `lenses: ["personas"]`, `summary` the personas in one line; per objection `claim`
the bet as a provable statement, `passage` a free label, `argument` the persona's reason, `proposal`,
`proof`.

## The bets

Risky objections, `[bet]` or `[gap]` left after a forge, `[open]` questions and the `Soft` and
`Breaks` items: one line each in the stack the engine returns. Gestures and calls as `.agentplugged/skills/critique-deliverable/references/gestures.md`, but offer two: **keep as
a hypothesis** (`hypothesis`) or **drop** (`discard`, with their reason); `fix` stays possible, you
change nothing without their say. A kept bet comes back with `hypotheses_due`; close it with their words only. Each report is sorted before it is made again (`critique_unsorted`). On a release
(`kind: prfaq`) the `passage` is a heading of `prfaq.md` (`Release`, `Customer FAQ`, `Internal
FAQ`), checked; on the idea, a free label.

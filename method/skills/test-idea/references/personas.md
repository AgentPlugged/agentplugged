# Personas

Compose 3 to 5 personas who would sink this idea, chosen for it, not from a fixed list: a
demanding customer, the competitor, a skeptical investor, a user in a hurry, whoever pays or
decides, someone who must trust it with their data. Give each a name, a stake and a question.

Played in turn, in a fresh context, each persona attacks the idea in its own voice:

1. Say what you would need to see to say yes. Say why you would walk away.
2. Keep two or three objections the idea gives no answer to. Order by damage.

Then, you:

- Merge the attacks: the **strong objections** (several personas, or fatal ones), **what holds**
  (attacks the idea answers), and the **risky bets** (what must be true and nobody knows yet).
- Show the creator that synthesis, a few lines. The bets become the report.

Report: `review.critique` as `references/format.md` of the skill `critique-deliverable`
(`.agentplugged/skills/critique-deliverable/references/format.md`) says, with `kind: idea`,
`lenses: ["personas"]`, `summary` the personas in one line. Per objection: `claim` the bet as a
statement that can be proved or refuted, `passage` a short label of the part of the idea it
aims at (not checked), `argument` the persona's reason, `proposal` and `proof` (what would
show it true or false: an interview, a test, a number).

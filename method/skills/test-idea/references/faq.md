# The two lists of questions

Play the reader who has been let down before. Show all the questions, then settle the answers one at a time. Each list holds **6 to 10 questions**, each opening with its angle:
`- [safety] Where does my data live? — answer`. The engine checks every angle is asked. A question
the creator cannot answer also gets `[open]`. In French use `[changer]`, `[sûreté]`, `[effort]`,
`[rare]`, `[redouté]`; `[construire]`, `[durer]`, `[moyens]`, `[danger]`, `[pourquoi-nous]`, `[évité]`.

**Customer FAQ** (`## Customer FAQ`): `[switch]` why this instead of the current way; `[safety]` data,
who stands behind it; `[effort]` time to start, price or upkeep; `[rare]` an unusual case;
`[dreaded]` the question the team wishes nobody would ask.

**Internal FAQ** (`## Internal FAQ`): `[build]` the hardest part; `[last]` how it endures and the
first users arrive; `[means]` who, how long, what is dropped; `[danger]` what kills it; `[why-us]`
why this team, why now; `[avoided]` what the creator steers around.

Adapt to the concept kind (`forge.md`). Answers are honest ("not yet" is allowed), concrete; "we'll see" is not one: ask what would settle it and by when. A gap an answer exposes: they class it
in their words, fix before launch, soon after, or accept.

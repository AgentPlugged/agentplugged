# The two lists of questions

Play the reader who has been let down before, not a fan. Ask what stands between interest and a
yes. Show all the questions together, then settle the answers one at a time.

Each list holds **6 to 10 questions**, each opening with its angle in brackets:
`- [safety] Where does my data live? — answer`. The engine checks that every angle is asked
(`docs check`). A question that cannot be answered from what they said gets a second tag, `[open]`.
In French use the French word (`[changer]`, `[sûreté]`, `[effort]`, `[rare]`, `[redouté]`;
`[construire]`, `[durer]`, `[moyens]`, `[danger]`, `[pourquoi-nous]`, `[évité]`).

**Customer FAQ** (`## Customer FAQ`):
- `[switch]` why this instead of what they use now.
- `[safety]` their data, who stands behind it, what happens if it stops.
- `[effort]` time to start, price or upkeep, what it plugs into.
- `[rare]` a real but unusual case, a neighbouring use.
- `[dreaded]` the question the team wishes nobody would put. Find it and ask it.

**Internal FAQ** (`## Internal FAQ`):
- `[build]` the hardest part; what nobody knows how to do yet.
- `[last]` how it pays or endures, how the first users arrive, why it holds against the others.
- `[means]` who, for how long; what is dropped to make room.
- `[danger]` what kills it, the worst outcome, legal exposure.
- `[why-us]` why this team, why now; what it takes from other work.
- `[avoided]` what the creator steers around.

Adapt to `concept.md`: for a tool of one's own or an open project, price becomes effort to adopt,
rivals become the current way, the company's survival becomes upkeep. A solo creator and a team are
not asked the same things.

**Answers** are honest ("not yet" is allowed), concrete (no "enterprise-grade") and believable.
"We'll see" is not an answer: ask what would settle it and by when. An answer that exposes a gap:
name it and let them class it in their words, to fix before launch, soon after, or to accept. They
can add questions; they know the scary ones.

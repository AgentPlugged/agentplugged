# The verdict

Say where the concept stands, plainly, in a last section `## Verdict` of `prfaq.md`. Three lines, one
per category; the engine checks them.

```
- Holds: what is clear, honest and defensible.
- Soft: what is promising but thin → what would firm it up.
- Breaks: a real risk, a contradiction or a hole → what facing it would take.
```

- Each item of `Soft` and `Breaks` ends with `→` and a next move; several items go in bullets below
  the line. A category with nothing says `none`. Labels follow the document's language (`Tient`,
  `Fragile`, `Casse`).
- The call is the worst category that has items: say it in one sentence. A release that breaks is
  information, and cheaper now than later.
- Name what stood up to the questions and what only just did; no score.
- Check that `concept:` is in the header: a closed release without it is refused.

Then:
1. The `Soft` and `Breaks` items and the `[open]` questions become bets: report them (`bets.md`).
2. They may reopen any part; rewrite the verdict afterwards.
3. Ask for the `document` checkpoint. The brief stays the framing document; the release is the base
   of the positioning.

Never decide for them what to do about a crack.

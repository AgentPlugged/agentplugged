# First principles

Strip the document to what must be true, then see what it added by habit.

1. State the need in one sentence, as the person who has it would, with no solution in it.
2. List what is physically, legally or logically fixed, and what is only convention or copied
   from how others do it.
3. Rebuild the simplest answer from the fixed things alone. Compare with the document: what does
   it carry that the need does not require, and what does the need require that it lacks?

Each gap is an objection: the extra weight or the missing piece is the `claim`, where the
document carries or omits it is the `passage`, and the simplest test that settles it is the `proof`.

# Steelman

Before attacking a choice, build the strongest case for the option the document rejected or
never mentioned. If the document still wins, it earned it.

1. Find the three or four biggest choices (a scope cut, a technology, a flow, a price).
2. For each, name the best alternative and argue for it as its most convinced defender would,
   with the real reasons.
3. Keep the choices that lose to that case, or that the document defends with a weaker reason
   than the alternative has. Say what the document leaves out.

Each is an objection: the better alternative is the `claim`, the passage that chose otherwise is
the `passage`, and what would show which is right (a measure, a trial) is the `proof`.

# Judgment of the whole

Judge the document as a stranger would, not as its author. The grid is in the checklist of the
skill that writes it: `write-prd/references/prd-checklist.md` ("Judgment of the whole") or
`write-ux-spec/references/ux-checklist.md` ("Excess").

1. Read the document, then the grid, and give each question a verdict (holds, thin or broken)
   with the passage that earns it.
2. Report only the thin and the broken. A question that holds needs no finding. Calibrate to
   the stakes: a personal tool is not judged like a public launch.
3. Order by what it costs whoever builds on the document.

Each is an objection: the verdict is the `claim`, the passage is the `passage`, and the question
a builder or the creator could not answer from the text is the `proof`.

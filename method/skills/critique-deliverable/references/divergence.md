# Divergence

Two teams build two parts of the product, each obeying every decision of the document exactly
as written. Find where the parts still do not fit.

1. Pick two parts that must work together (two modules, a client and a server, two screens that
   share data).
2. Build each in your head, applying each decision as written and nothing else. Wherever a choice
   is left open (a format, an owner, a name, an error, an order), the two builders choose
   differently.
3. Keep the divergences that would break the whole: a seam nobody owns, a rule that says what to
   do but not how far, a decision left to "later" that both parts depend on.

Each is an objection: the divergence is the `claim`, the decision (`AD-n`) that should have
closed it is the `passage`, and the integration test that would fail is the `proof`.

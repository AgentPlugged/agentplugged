# Advocate against

Defend the opposite choice with conviction, as someone who would lose if the document were
followed and who is intelligent and fair.

1. List the document's main choices (what is in, what is out, how it works, in what order).
2. For each, build the strongest case for the other choice: who it serves better, what it costs
   less, what the document's choice gives up.
3. Keep the cases the document does not answer. A choice that survives its best opponent is
   not an objection.

Each unanswered case is an objection: the case is the `claim`, the choice it contests is the
`passage`, and what would show which choice is right is the `proof`.

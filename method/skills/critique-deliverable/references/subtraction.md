# Subtraction

Strengthen the document by cutting. What can go without anything breaking?

1. List every requirement, screen, section, option and detail.
2. For each, ask what is lost if it is removed: a journey, a promise of the brief, a test. If
   nothing is lost, it is a candidate. Also note what repeats another passage, and what fixes a
   value (a pixel, a version) where a rule would do.
3. Keep the candidates that cost something to build, read or maintain. Order by that cost.

Each is an objection: "this can go" is the `claim`, the passage is the `passage`, and what
would show it is not missed (nobody asks, a journey still works) is the `proof`.

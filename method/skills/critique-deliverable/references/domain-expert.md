# Domain expert

Read the document as a veteran of the field it enters would, one who has seen similar products
fail for reasons outsiders miss.

1. Name the field in one line (health data, logistics, teaching, payments, games…) and the
   rules, habits and traps of it that every practitioner knows.
2. Walk the document and mark what that person would find naive, impossible, illegal, or already
   solved a known way.
3. Keep what is wrong for the field, not what is merely unusual. Say what the practitioner
   would do.

Each is an objection: what the practitioner would say is the `claim`, the passage is the
`passage`, and a real practitioner's answer or a standard of the field is the `proof`.

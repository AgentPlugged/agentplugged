# Inversion

Instead of asking how to succeed, ask how to guarantee failure, then check the document does not
do it.

1. Write the shortest list of things that would surely make this product fail (users leave,
   data is lost, nobody pays, the team stalls).
2. For each, look in the document for what it does that moves toward that failure, or fails to
   prevent it.
3. Keep what the document does or allows. Order by how directly it leads to the failure.

Each is an objection: the sure way to fail is the `claim`, the passage that leans toward it is
the `passage`, and what would show it is happening is the `proof`.

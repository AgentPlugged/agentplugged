# Assumption audit

List what the document takes for true without having checked it, and test the weakest.

1. Extract the assumptions, stated (`[assumption]`, "we expect") or silent (about users, volumes,
   platforms, partners, the team).
2. Rank them by how likely each is to be wrong and by what it costs the product if it is.
3. Take the few at the top. For each, say what the document would have to change if it fell.

Each is an objection: the assumption is the `claim`, the passage that rests on it is the
`passage`, and the cheapest check (a question, a number, a prototype) is the `proof`.

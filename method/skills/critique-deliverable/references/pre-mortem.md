# Pre-mortem

The product shipped and failed. Say it is a year from now: the document was followed to the
letter, and the product is a failure. You are writing the post-mortem.

1. Write the three to five most likely causes of the failure, each as a sentence in the past tense.
2. For each, find the passage of the document that made it possible: a requirement missing, a
   promise too broad, a figure never checked, a user nobody considered.
3. Keep the causes the document could have prevented. Order by how likely and how costly.

Each cause is an objection: the cause is the `claim`, the passage that allowed it is the
`passage`, and what would show it coming is the `proof`.

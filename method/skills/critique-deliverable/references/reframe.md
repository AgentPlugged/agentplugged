# Reframe the question

Perhaps the document answers the wrong question well. Ask it again in other terms.

1. State the problem the document solves in one sentence, as it does.
2. Restate it three ways: wider (the goal behind the goal), narrower (the smallest part that
   hurts), and from the user's side (what they are trying to get done, not what they are given).
3. Check whether the document's answer still stands in each. Keep the framings under which a
   simpler or different answer wins.

Each is an objection: the better question is the `claim`, the passage that fixed the first
framing is the `passage`, and what the user would say they wanted is the `proof`.

# Extreme user

Test the document against the users at its edges, since the middle is what it was written for.

1. Choose three or four: the most expert, the newest, the one with the most data, the one with
   the least time or the worst connection, the one who does not read, the one who abuses.
2. For each, walk their journey through the document and mark where it stops, wastes their
   time, locks them out or breaks.
3. Keep the gaps a real share of users will meet. Drop the rare ones the product may ignore, and
   say so.

Each is an objection: what happens to that user is the `claim`, the requirement or flow they
cross is the `passage`, and a session with that kind of user is the `proof`.

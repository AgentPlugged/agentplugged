# The spec: parts, scope and growth

## Writing it

Copy the template to `.agentplugged/docs/spec.md`; keep the header keys and the part markers;
remove the guidance comments, the "Generated by agentplugged" line and every `<…>` placeholder.
Each `EF-n` is testable, each `ENF-n` has a threshold, each `AD-n` states Binds, Prevents and Rule,
every `EF-n` is in a story line. A choice between real options goes to a `decision` checkpoint,
never settled in the creator's place. The header holds the scope and no status, cost or progress.

## The four parts

Each part opens with its marker (`part: brief`, `requirements`, `decisions` or `stories`, as an
HTML comment on its own line, before the heading) and stays short: a spec that needs more than
about 15 functional requirements or 8 stories is no longer a short spec.

- **Brief:** the product, its users, the problem, what is out of scope. The creator's words.
- **Requirements:** `EF-n` (one testable sentence), `ENF-n` (a threshold and how it is measured).
  When the scope names personal data, a payment or accounts, write the security requirements it
  calls for as `ENF-n` (who may read what, where secrets live, what is logged): the register of
  requirements and the security reviews read them there.
- **Decisions:** `AD-n`, only what binds every story, each with its three fields (Binds,
  Prevents, Rule).
- **Stories:** one line each, `1-1 : title (EF-1, ENF-2)`; the epic is the first number.

## The scope in the header

`size` (small, medium, large), `users` (a number, the first year), `sensitive` (a list among
`personal_data`, `payment`, `accounts`; `[]` when none), `integrations` (external services),
`platforms`. They are the creator's answers, never your estimate. A product with something in
`sensitive` takes the short path only if the creator asked for it: `asked: short` in the header,
and `docs check` refuses it otherwise (`SPEC_SCOPE`). Never lower a risk to keep the short path.

## Growing

`state.next` says `spec_path: grow` (with `spec_path_why`) when the scope or the size of the spec
calls for the full path. Noah offers it in one line; the creator decides. When they accept, the
full path is on again (`step_resume` of `full_path`) and `npx agentplugged docs grow` writes the
brief, the PRD and the architecture from the spec's parts, word for word, identifiers kept, and
removes the spec. Nothing is retyped; the new documents are validated like any others.

## The readiness gate

The spec passes the gate too, in its light form (`track: short`): the engine checks it is validated
and unchanged, its identifiers known and each requirement carried by a story; Izzy reads the spec and
the stories for contradictions and gaps, six findings at most (skill `check-readiness`). Noah
proposes it from `state.next` `readiness`, before the stories are cut or built.

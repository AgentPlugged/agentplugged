# The spec

## Writing it

Copy the template to `.agentplugged/docs/spec.md`; keep the header keys and part markers; remove the
guidance comments, the "Generated by agentplugged" line, every `<…>` placeholder. Each `EF-n` is
testable, each `ENF-n` has a threshold, each `AD-n` states Binds, Prevents and Rule, every `EF-n` is
in a story line. A choice between real options goes to a `decision` checkpoint. The header holds the
scope and no status, cost or progress.

## The four parts

Each part opens with its marker (`part: brief`, `requirements`, `decisions` or `stories`, an HTML
comment on its own line before the heading); more than about 15 functional requirements or 8
stories is no longer a short spec.

- **Brief:** the product, its users, the problem, what is out of scope, in their words.
- **Requirements:** `EF-n` (one testable sentence), `ENF-n` (a threshold and how it is measured).
  When the scope names personal data, a payment or accounts, write the security requirements it
  calls for as `ENF-n` (who reads what, where secrets live, what is logged).
- **Decisions:** `AD-n`, only what binds every story, each with its three fields (Binds,
  Prevents, Rule).
- **Stories:** one line each, `1-1 : title (EF-1, ENF-2)`.

## The scope in the header

`size` (small, medium, large), `users` (first year), `sensitive` (a list among
`personal_data`, `payment`, `accounts`; `[]` when none), `integrations` (external services),
`platforms`. They are the creator's answers, never your estimate. A product with something in
`sensitive` takes the short path only if the creator asked for it: `asked: short` in the header,
and `docs check` refuses it otherwise (`SPEC_SCOPE`). Never lower a risk to keep it.

## Growing

`state.next` says `spec_path: grow` (with `spec_path_why`) when the scope or size calls for the
full path. Noah offers it; the creator decides. If they accept, the full path is on again (`step_resume` of `full_path`) and `npx agentplugged docs grow` writes the
brief, the PRD and the architecture from the spec's parts, word for word, identifiers kept, and
removes the spec. Nothing is retyped; the new documents are validated as usual.

## The readiness gate

The spec passes the gate too (`track: short`): the engine checks it is validated and unchanged, its
identifiers known and each requirement carried by a story; Izzy reads the spec and the stories, six
findings at most (skill `check-readiness`). Noah proposes it from `state.next` `readiness`.

# Positioning checklist

Walk through it before showing the positioning to the creator. A failed line is fixed, or it
stays visible as an open question.

## Grounding

- Every claim of the promise and of the difference has a row in **Evidence**.
- Each `verified` row names a page of the knowledge base that says it, not the repository, not
  memory, and that page is current (`npx agentplugged next --json` gave no knowledge base
  update).
- Each `stated` row carries the date of the creator's statement and is not worded as proven.
- Each `untrusted` row names the source it was read from and never carries the weight of a
  verified claim: it is a lead the creator confirms, not a promise.
- No figure, quote, customer, award or comparison appears that no row backs.
- What the product cannot do, or does only in part, is not promised; the limits the base states
  are respected.
- A fact the base lacks is a question or a `decision` checkpoint, never a guess.

## Content

- **Audience:** one audience first, described by their situation and their own words.
- **Problem:** told from the reader's side, with the words they search with; features come
  second.
- **Promise:** one promise the product can keep, in two variants with their reasons; the
  creator chose.
- **Difference:** each line says what this product does differently from a real alternative; no
  line is only an adjective.
- The five sections exist, once, and none is empty (`docs check` verifies it).

## Form

- Written in the product's language, headings in the creator's; `language` names the product's.
- Header: `id: positioning`, `kind: note`, `owner: julien`, `links` naming at least one document
  the positioning is drawn from, `language`. No status, no cost, no progress.
- No requirement identifier opens a line, a list item, a heading or a first table cell.
- No guidance comment, no "Generated by agentplugged" line and no placeholder is left.
- `npx agentplugged docs check` reports no problem for it.

## Validation

- The positioning is committed, and its `publication` checkpoint cites the committed blob.
- It is ready for the creator to validate; it is validated only once that checkpoint is resolved
  `approved` or `approved_with_reservations` for that very version.

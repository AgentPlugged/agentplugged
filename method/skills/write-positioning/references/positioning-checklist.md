# Positioning checklist

Before showing the positioning. A failed line is fixed or stays an open question.

- Every claim of the promise and the difference has a row in **Evidence**. `verified` names a
  current base page (not the repository, not memory); `stated` carries the creator's date;
  `untrusted` names its source and is a lead, never a promise.
- No figure, quote, customer, award or comparison that no row backs; nothing promised that the
  product cannot do; a missing fact is a question or a `decision`.
- **Audience:** one first, by their situation and words. **Problem:** the reader's side, with the
  words they search with. **Promise:** one the product can keep, two variants, the creator chose.
  **Difference:** against a real alternative, never only an adjective. Five sections, once, none
  empty.
- Header `id: positioning`, `kind: note`, `owner: julien`, `links` to one document at least,
  `language`; no status or cost; no identifier opening a line; no comment, generated line or
  placeholder; `npx agentplugged docs check` clean.
- Committed, and its `publication` cites the committed blob; validated only once resolved
  `approved` or `approved_with_reservations` for that very version.

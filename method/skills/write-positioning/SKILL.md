## Overview

- **Step:** launch, as Julien (`agent: julien` in every call).
- **Produces:** the positioning (`assets/positioning-template.md`,
  `.agentplugged/docs/launch/positioning.md`), then, once validated, page texts
  (`assets/page-template.md`, `.agentplugged/docs/launch/pages/<slug>.md`). Later work never redefines
  the positioning.
- **Reads:** the base (`index.md` first), the brief, the PRD (`docs read`).
- **Validation point:** a `publication` checkpoint per deliverable.

## Before you start

- Julien not active in `state.get`, or `marketing` off: say so, `partial`.
- Text in the **product's language** (header `language`), the rest in the creator's.

## Decisions

A fact the base lacks is never guessed. **Open:** `checkpoint.open`, `type: decision`, options
`provide`, `ask_base`, `leave_out`; **mark** `[open: G-…]` at the sentence's end. **Apply:** once resolved, read its `resolution` in full (`state.get { checkpoint: "<id>" }`) and apply it as "Applying a resolution" says (reference `skill-rules`); remove the mark; a creator's statement goes in **Evidence** as `stated`, with its date.

## Steps

1. **Start.** `agent.status` `working`; `state.get`; `npx agentplugged next --json`. Phase not
   `launch`, or `next` names another step: say so, `partial`.
2. **Read what exists.** Without `index.md` there is no knowledge base: Zoé writes it, `partial`.
   The press release (`prfaq.md`) when the creator validated it: its release is the first draft of
   the promise. Start from it, never copy it: a claim still needs its row in **Evidence**. Add its id
   (`prfaq`) to `links`, likewise `launch/narrative.md` (`narrative`) once validated. Intent: **positioning**, **pages** — the positioning is **validated**
   (step 10), else write nothing; **review** (`references/positioning-checklist.md`,
   `references/seo-checklist.md`, `docs check`, `partial`; never copy a template nor open a
   checkpoint). After a refusal read its `checkpoint.resolution.note`.
3. **Ground the claims** in the base; ask the creator's facts once (dated). What remains missing
   and weighs becomes a `decision`.
4. **Write the positioning** from the template, without the "Generated by agentplugged" line and
   comments. Header `id: positioning`, `owner: julien`, `links` (one document at least),
   `language`. Sections **Audience**, **Problem**, **Promise** (two phrasings, the creator
   chooses), **Difference** (nothing the base cannot prove), **Evidence** (a row per claim:
   `verified`, `stated` with its date, or `untrusted`; no row, no claim).
5. **Review gate (positioning).** `references/positioning-checklist.md`, then `docs check`
   until clean.
6. **Pages,** only when validated. A product without a website has no page in this folder: write
   the positioning only. Per page: intent; keywords per `references/seo-checklist.md`
   (`hypothesis: true` without volume, source and date; never a volume from memory); reading the web
   with `guard.external_read` (`tool`, `source`): declare the read first, then read, and what you
   take stays marked `untrusted`; title and description (lengths in the checklist); text on the positioning only; a goal.
7. **Review gate (pages).** `references/seo-checklist.md`, `docs check` until clean.
8. **Decision gate.** Every `decision` you opened resolved; else no publication, `partial`.
9. **Commit and open the publication.** `git branch --show-current` names a branch (else ask, open
   nothing); `git commit` the deliverable; `checkpoint.open` `type: publication`, no story, low
   risk (positioning) or medium (page), `documents` the deliverable alone (`path`, `branch`,
   `blob_sha` from `git rev-parse HEAD:<path>`). One checkpoint per deliverable: the positioning,
   then each page; `supersedes` after a refusal. Ask once: `chat.answer`.
10. **Validated.** The last `publication` checkpoint citing the positioning is `approved` or
    `approved_with_reservations` (never a waiver, never an option picked), its `blob_sha` is the
    blob of the file as it is now (`git rev-parse HEAD:<path>` equal to it, committed). A draft, an
    open or refused checkpoint, a file edited since: not validated. Else pages can follow
    (`PG`); run `npx agentplugged next --json`; never open the end of the launch.
11. **Report.** `story.result` (`complete` once the publication is open, else `partial`).

## Rules

- Follow "Rules for skills and agents" (reference `skill-rules`): checkpoint before the question, ask once; a point the creator did not keep goes to `checkpoint.decide`; in French say « tu »; start with `memory.read`.
- Write only `.agentplugged/docs/launch/positioning.md` and the files of `.agentplugged/docs/launch/pages/`.
- Never publish, send, schedule, buy or pay anything.
- Never invent a fact, figure, quote, customer, competitor's weakness or search volume.
- Declare every read outside the product with `guard.external_read` before you read; it stays `untrusted`.
- Never call another agent: hand back to Noah.

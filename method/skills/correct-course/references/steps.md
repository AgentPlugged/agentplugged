# Correcting the course, step by step

1. `agent.status` (`working`), under your own id. Say the change in one line and name what it touches
   (`starts.md`); read the document or `state.trace` before naming, never guess.
2. `plan.course` `step: open` (`summary`, `starts`, their words in `quote`). Show `stack` as it
   is, then `blocked` (their developers are told, they do not merge) and `proofs`.
3. One gesture per element, in their words (`gestures.md`): `step: decide`.
4. Do your own updates (PRD: `write-prd` « Identifiers » and « Decisions »; then
   `npx agentplugged docs check`; a `decision` checkpoint for each ambiguity the change brings);
   hand the others over with `handoff`.
5. `step: close`: fix what `problems` says, close again. `agent.status` (`idle`), hand back to Noah.

Never decide a gesture for the creator, rewrite their text without their gesture, renumber an
identifier or delete one: retire it.

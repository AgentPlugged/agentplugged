# Lens: prose

For documents (brief, requirements, UX, architecture) and the words of the interface.

- One idea per sentence; no word the reader must guess; the same thing always called the same.
- Claims with a number, a name or a source; none that sound sure and cannot be checked.
- Contradictions inside the document or with the documents it cites.
- Tone and language: the creator's, plain, no jargon left unexplained.

Quote the passage. Do not rewrite the document; say what is wrong and where. Report shape:
`format.md`.

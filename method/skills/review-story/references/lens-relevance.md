# Lens: relevance

Does the change belong here?

- Scope: does every changed file serve the story's intent and criteria? What is outside its
  `Out of scope`, or not asked for?
- The project's rules: the requirements it cites (`EF-n`, `ENF-n`, compare with the
  `check-requirements` skill: a drift is also a deviation, `story.deviation`), the decisions
  `AD-n`, the conventions in the knowledge base.
- Rot: dead code, a second way to do what exists, a workaround that hides the cause, a growing
  exception, a dependency added without need.

Quote the rule or decision a finding breaks. Answer shape: `format.md`.

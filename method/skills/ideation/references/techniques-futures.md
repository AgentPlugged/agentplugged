# Futures: move the horizon

- Letter from ahead: write the note a user sends in ten years about what the product became. Use to make a distant future concrete.
- Backcasting: fix the end state you want, then step backwards to today, one dependency at a time. Use when the goal is clear but the path is not.
- Trend to the extreme: take one trend and push it until it breaks or reverses; what grows around the break? Use to find what lasts.
- Four worlds: cross two uncertain forces as axes and give each quarter a name and an idea. Use for a long bet with no single likely future.
- Ground moves: the platform you rely on changes its terms or closes; how does the product live on? Use to test how well it would survive.
- Rules change: a law, a price or a habit shifts overnight; what becomes possible? Use to spot an opening before others.

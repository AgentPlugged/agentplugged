# Borrowed: take it from elsewhere

- Nature's answer: how does an organism handle this (ants, a forest, an immune system)? Use for coordination, growth or defence problems.
- Ecosystem: roles, niches, who depends on whom and who feeds on whom. Use for platforms, communities and markets.
- Selection: apply a strong pressure and keep the variants that survive. Use to turn many options into a few fit ones.
- Swarm: no leader, simple local rules; which rules would produce the behaviour you want? Use for self-organising products.
- Ritual: what habit or ceremony does the product create, replace or break? Use to design for habit, not feature.
- Craft: how would a craftsperson of an old trade do it by hand? Use to recover care and quality.

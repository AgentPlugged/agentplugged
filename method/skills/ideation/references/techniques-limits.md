# Limits: a constraint is a prompt

- Nothing to spend: no money at all; what is left? Use when money is the habit of the answer.
- Unlimited means: nothing is scarce; what would you build? Use to find the real ambition.
- Single purpose: the product may do one thing; which, and how well? Use to find the core.
- A tenth of the scope: cut everything to a tenth; what stays? Use to find what proves the idea.
- No explanation: it must be usable with no instructions at all. Use when the idea feels heavy to learn.
- Pushed to the limit: take one quality (speed, simplicity, price, size) to the extreme. Use to find a sharp edge.
- Hostile setting: no connection, no screen, one hand, a noisy room; design for it. Use when ideas dry up.

# Together: turns and building on each other

Fit `exchange` and `proposal`; in `questions` you set the rule and they do the writing.

- Yes, and: each turn accepts the last idea and lifts it one rung. Use at the start, or when energy dips.
- Silent writing: they write five ideas alone, then build on their own list in a second pass. Use when first ideas crowd each other.
- Word relay: each new idea must reuse one word of the one before. Use when the pace is slow or the ideas loop.
- Copy and twist: take something that already exists and change a single property of it. Use when a blank page paralyses.
- Persuade the doubter: you play a sceptic; each answer they give to win you over is an idea. Use when they hold a view they have not tested.
- Many voices: speak in turn as the user, the rival, the investor, the doubter; each says what they want and fear. Use when the view is one-sided.
- Second wind: after a break, return to the weakest of the first ten ideas and extend it. Use when the early ideas were dropped too fast.

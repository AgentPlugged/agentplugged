# Techniques and how to choose

A batch is 3 or 4 techniques. The creator picks how, once; do not list the library.
**They name it:** run it as named. **Recommended:** from the goal and how stuck they are, say in one
line each why it fits; techniques that worked in the recent notes of `ideas/` come first. **Drawn:**
a blind pick, say it was drawn. **A chain:** gentle to hard (`together` or `frames`, `sparks`,
`limits` or `futures`, last `deep`), order said. **Invented:** name and principle in a
line, written under `### <name> (new)`. A batch run dry: ask in one line, more techniques, narrow down, or wrap up.

Goal to category: cold start `together`, `sparks`; unclear problem `deep`, `frames`; too safe
`limits`, `futures`; users unknown `people`; stuck in the trade's way `borrowed`, `sparks`.

- `together` (`exchange`, `proposal`; in `questions` you set the rule): yes, and; silent writing; word relay; copy and twist; persuade the doubter; many voices; second wind.
- `sparks`: what if; analogy; ground rules; forced pairing; name game; image swap; blend; senses.
- `frames`: SCAMPER; six hats; eight sketches; how might we; job to be done; empathy map; mind map; starbursting; storyboard; cross grid.
- `deep` (for a full diagnosis, the skill `strategy`): five whys; question storm; hidden rules; pre-mortem; provocation; zoom; contradiction hunt; grid of options.
- `limits`: nothing to spend; unlimited means; single purpose; a tenth of the scope; no explanation; pushed to the limit; hostile setting.
- `futures`: letter from ahead; backcasting; trend to the extreme; four worlds; ground moves; rules change.
- `people`: day in the life; extreme users; stranger's eye; rival's seat; customer's letter.
- `borrowed`: nature's answer; ecosystem; selection; swarm; ritual; craft.

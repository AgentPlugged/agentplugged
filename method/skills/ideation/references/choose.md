# Choosing the techniques

A batch is 3 or 4 techniques. The creator picks how, once, in one line; do not list the library.

- **They name it.** A technique or a category: run it as named.
- **Recommended.** Choose from the goal and how stuck they are, say in one line each why it fits. The goal
  and the categories (files `references/techniques-<category>.md`):
  - nothing comes, a cold start: `together`, `sparks`
  - the problem itself is unclear: `deep`, `frames`
  - ideas are too safe: `limits`, `futures`
  - the users are not well known: `people`
  - stuck in the usual way of the trade: `borrowed`, `sparks`
  Techniques that worked in the recent notes of `ideas/` come first.
- **Drawn.** A blind pick, no taste: take the category they name (or the next one in the table above), and
  the line whose rank is the number of ideas so far, counting from the top and wrapping round. Say it was drawn.
- **A chain.** Start gentle, end hard: `together` or `frames`, then `sparks`, then `limits` or `futures`,
  last `deep` to test what stands. Say the order up front.
- **Invented.** On the spot, in the spirit of a category: say its name and principle in a line, run it,
  and write it under `### <name> (new)`.

Once a batch has run dry, ask in one line: more techniques, narrow down, or wrap up.

# Closing the session

1. **Their ideas back to them.** Under `## Mirror`, a lively selection of the creator's own ideas,
   copied as written (three at least, or all if fewer; the engine counts), the strange, the stray and
   the forgotten ones too. Note what they notice on lines starting `Noted:`.
2. **Then the bridges they did not see.** Lines beginning `Link:`: no fresh ideas, only unexpected
   ties. In `questions` this is where your own thinking comes in, labelled as a link.
3. **What stays.** Under `## Kept`, one bullet per idea they retain, in their words, ending with `→`
   and the gesture they pick; if nothing stays, one plain line saying so.
4. **Wrap up.** Remove `Next step:`, run the docs check, hand back to Noah.

## Gestures

Ask once, after the mirror, one gesture per kept idea. Never choose for them.
- **hypothesis**: the idea rests on a bet to prove. Call and fields as in
  `.agentplugged/skills/strategy/references/gestures.md`, "Hypothesis followed": one finding per bet,
  its `passage` citing the `EF-n` or `AD-n` the idea touches (none yet: the id of the session file).
- **story**: worth building. Hand it to Noah (requirement: Maya; story: Izzy). Write neither.
- **decision**: a choice between two to four kept ideas only they can make: `checkpoint.open`,
  `type: decision`, options, your recommendation, the session file in `documents`.
- **nothing**: it stays in the file. **later**: not decided yet.

# Closing the session

1. **Their ideas back to them.** Under `## Mirror`, return a lively selection of the creator's own
   ideas, copied as written (three at least, or all of them if fewer; the engine counts), taking the
   strange, the stray and the forgotten ones from early in the session, not just the latest. Ask what
   they notice: patterns, ties, the few that count. They draw the connections first; note what they
   say in their words on lines starting `Noted:`, so a cut session keeps it.
2. **Then the bridges they did not see.** Add lines beginning `Link:`: no fresh ideas, only unexpected
   ties (this one answers that tension; these three are the same idea). In the `questions` stance this
   is the single spot where your own thinking comes in, and it stays labelled as a link. What to do with
   it is theirs to say.
3. **What stays.** Under `## Kept`, one bullet per idea they retain, in their words, ending with `→`
   and the gesture they pick: `references/gestures.md`. If nothing stays, write one plain line saying so,
   with no bullet: that is their choice too.
4. **Wrap up.** Remove the `Next step:` line, run the docs check, fix what it reports. Offer once what
   could follow: a new session on one kept idea, or the table (Noah opens it) when an idea needs other
   minds; then hand back to Noah.

# People: see through other eyes

- Day in the life: walk one user through a day, step by step, and note each friction. Use when the journey is unknown.
- Extreme users: a beginner, an expert, someone in a hurry, someone who cannot use their hands. Use to find what the average hides.
- Stranger's eye: a visitor with no context watches the product in use; what puzzles them? Use to see what you stopped seeing.
- Rival's seat: you run the competitor and have to beat this product; how? Use to find the weak side, then fix it.
- Customer's letter: write the review of a delighted customer, then of an angry one. Use to find what to promise and what to avoid.

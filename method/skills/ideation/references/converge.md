# Narrowing down

Only once the ideas stop coming or the creator asks to choose; say the mode changes.
Never during a technique that still yields, never inside a batch. The stance carries over: in
`questions` they decide and you rank nothing; in `exchange` your view is marked as yours; in
`proposal` you may offer a first scoring.

1. Lay out every live candidate, the strange and forgotten ones included; say how many.
2. Pick one tool for this goal and name it, no menu: group by theme; impact and effort; new, useful,
   feasible (1 to 10); forced ranking; plus, minus, interesting; must, should, could, not now.
3. Record it under `## Convergence`: a line `Move: <name>` per tool, then the outcome. A second
   tool may follow the first; a third is refused.
4. Stop when a short list or one direction stands out. The creator says which ideas stay, in their
   words; you carry them to `## Kept` (`references/close.md`).

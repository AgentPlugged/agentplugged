# From kept ideas to gestures

Ask once, after the mirror, one gesture per kept idea. They may answer some now and the rest later
(`later`). Never choose for them. The line of the idea in `## Kept` records it.

- **hypothesis**: a kept idea that rests on a bet to prove (users will want it, it can be built, it will
  pay). The call, the fields and the creator's sorting are those of `.agentplugged/skills/strategy/references/gestures.md`,
  "Hypothesis followed": here one finding per bet, its `passage` citing the `EF-n` or `AD-n` the idea touches
  (with no requirement yet, the id of the session file).
- **story**: the idea is worth building. Your closing line hands it to Noah: a new requirement goes to
  Maya, a story to cut to Izzy. Write neither.
- **decision**: a choice between two to four kept ideas that only they can make. `checkpoint.open` with
  `type: decision`, the options, your recommended one and the session file in `documents`. Record the answer in
  the file once it is given.
- **nothing**: the idea stays in the file, nothing follows. **later**: not decided yet.

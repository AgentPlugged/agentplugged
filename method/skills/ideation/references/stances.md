# The stance

Ask once, in a line, and write the answer after `Stance:`. The creator can change it: add a line `Stance: <new one>` in `## Ideas` at that point (earlier
ideas keep their marks).

- `Stance: questions`. You bring no idea of your own: you question, provoke, set constraints and
  read back. If they ask outright for one, offer a single seed, marked `[lou]` (the engine allows
  one per technique); a second request means the technique is used up, so switch. Mark every
  other idea `[creator]`.
- `Stance: exchange`. Say at the start how it works and that they steer: they can turn down any
  idea of yours. "Yes, and" a step further, then challenge them to go beyond; after one of yours,
  turn back to them with a question. If you have given more than they have lately, ease off. Mark
  each idea `[creator]` or `[lou]`.
- `Stance: proposal`. A first list per technique (ten ideas, each marked `[lou]`); they react and
  add theirs (`[creator]`). "You do it": run the batch alone, then offer `exchange`.

In every stance: write their idea word for word, never smoothed or merged; build on it before adding yours.

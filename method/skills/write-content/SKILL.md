## Overview

- **Step:** launch, as Julien (`agent: julien` in every call).
- **Produces:** an article or post (`assets/content-template.md`, to
  `.agentplugged/docs/launch/content/<slug>.md`) or a campaign plan (`assets/campaign-template.md`,
  to `.agentplugged/docs/launch/campaigns/<slug>.md`): one channel, one goal.
- **Reads:** the positioning, the base pages needed.
- **Validation point:** a `publication` checkpoint per deliverable.

## Before you start

- Julien not active in `state.get`, or the `marketing` domain off: say so, `partial`.
- Text in the language of **the audience of its channel**.

## Decisions

A fact the base lacks is never guessed. **Open:** `checkpoint.open`, `type: decision`, options
`provide`, `ask_base`, `leave_out`; **mark** `[open: G-…]` at the sentence's end. **Apply:** once resolved, read its `resolution` in full (`state.get { checkpoint: "<id>" }`) and apply it as "Applying a resolution" says (reference `skill-rules`); remove the mark; a creator's statement goes in `references` as `creator`.

## Steps

1. **Start.** `agent.status` `working`; `state.get`; `npx agentplugged next --json`; not
   `launch` or `next` elsewhere: say so, `partial`.
2. **The positioning must be validated** (not for a review): the last *approved* `publication`
   checkpoint citing `.agentplugged/docs/launch/positioning.md` (`approved` or
   `approved_with_reservations`; never a waiver, never an option picked) has a `blob_sha` that is
   the blob of the file as it is now in the working copy (`git rev-parse HEAD:<path>`). Else it is **not
   validated** (no file, a draft, an open checkpoint, a file edited since the approval): write
   nothing, open nothing, say which, `partial`.
3. **Read.** Without `index.md` there is no knowledge base: Zoé writes it,
   `partial`. After a refusal, read `checkpoint.resolution.note`.
4. **Settle the request**, one question at a time: the kind (article, post, campaign plan, or
   **review**: `references/content-checklist.md`, `docs check`, `partial`; never copy a template,
   never open a checkpoint); angle, audience, channel; the measurable goal (metric, target above
   zero, date, observation): no goal, no content. Never decide a budget.
5. **Ground the claims** in the base; the creator's facts, asked once, are dated. A missing fact
   that weighs becomes a `decision`, or is left out.
6. **Reading outside the product,** with the creator's agreement: `guard.external_read`
   (`tool`, `source`): declare the read first, then read. Attribute it (« according to
   <source>, read on <date> ») in `content.references` and **Sources** as `untrusted`, never a
   promise. Never invent or round a figure, quote or customer.
7. **Write an article or post** without the "Generated by agentplugged" line and comments. Header:
   `type`, `channel`, `positioning_blob` (read in step 2), `goal` (`metric`, `target`, `by`,
   `observed_with`), `references`, `language`, `links`: `positioning` and the id of each page of the
   base used. Text: two hooks; every figure or outside claim in it has its reference.
8. **Write a campaign plan** (`type: campaign_plan`): **Calendar** (the creator's actions; nothing is scheduled by you), **Budget** (the creator's
   amount, or « No budget: every action is free. ». A plan never spends), **Creator actions**
   (Julien does none of them).
9. **Review gate.** `references/content-checklist.md`, `npx agentplugged docs check` clean, no warning (`launch_content_positioning_outdated`: re-read, reset `positioning_blob`, new
   publication).
10. **Decision gate.** Every `decision` resolved, else `partial`.
11. **Commit and open the publication.** `git branch --show-current` names a branch (else ask, open
    nothing); `git commit`; `checkpoint.open` `type: publication`, no story, medium risk (high for
    a campaign plan whose budget is above zero), `documents` the deliverable alone (`path`,
    `branch`, `blob_sha` from `git rev-parse HEAD:<path>`). One checkpoint per deliverable;
    `supersedes` after a refusal. Ask once: `chat.answer`.
12. **Report.** `story.result` (`complete` once the publication is open, else `partial`).

## Rules

- Follow "Rules for skills and agents" (reference `skill-rules`): checkpoint before the question, ask once; a point the creator did not keep goes to `checkpoint.decide`; in French say « tu »; start with `memory.read`.
- Produce nothing without a validated positioning, and say so (step 2).
- Write only the files of `.agentplugged/docs/launch/content/` and `.agentplugged/docs/launch/campaigns/`.
- Never publish, send, schedule, book, buy or pay anything: the creator publishes, the creator spends.
- Never invent a fact, figure, quote or customer. A figure always has its source.
- Declare every read outside the product with `guard.external_read` before you read; keep it
  `untrusted`, never state it as the product's own claim.
- Link every content to the positioning and each base page it uses.
- Never open the end of the launch.
